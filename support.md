## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Rise → Cancel Subscription.

You can also tap "Manage subscription" in the Rise Settings tab.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the paywall (Settings → Upgrade) and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

### My alarm didn't ring
Check the following on your device:
1. **Silent mode (mute switch)** — iOS silences default alarm sounds when the side switch is on. Critical Alerts is required to override this; if your installation does not have that entitlement enabled, please ensure the mute switch is off.
2. **Do Not Disturb / Focus** — Rise alarms use the `.timeSensitive` interruption level, which most Focus modes pass through. Verify Rise is in your Focus allow-list.
3. **Notification permission** — Settings → Rise → Notifications → ensure Sounds and Banners are on.
4. **Low Power Mode** — can delay scheduled notifications by up to a few minutes; turn off the night before.

### The AI coach didn't talk to me
The wake-up conversation requires an internet connection. Try:
1. Check Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity
2. Force-quit Rise (swipe up) and reopen
3. If still failing, email us with the time the alarm fired and your iOS version

### How do I switch personas (Calm Coach / Hype Friend / Drill Sergeant)?
Settings tab → "Wake-up coach" → choose the persona. Switching invalidates the cached greeting audio and re-renders new greetings.

### How do I change the app language?
Settings tab → "Language" → choose English or Türkçe. The app re-renders without a restart.

### How does the streak work?
A "streak day" is a day on which you completed a wake-up conversation (alarm fired + you spoke a 3+ word intention). Missing a day resets the current streak; the longest streak you've ever held is preserved.

### My HealthKit sleep data isn't showing in Insights
Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Rise → ensure "Sleep Analysis" read access is on. The first time data appears can take 24-48h after granting permission, depending on what your sleep tracker writes to HealthKit.
